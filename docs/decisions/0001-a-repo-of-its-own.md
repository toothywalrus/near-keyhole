# 0001: Keyhole is its own repo; the design comes from near-lab

**Context.** near-lab's `0010` and `docs/ideas/keyhole.md` chose this design for Illia Polosukhin's Nov 4 idea:
"private trading where you sell your viewing keys ... PnL can be publicly displayed so people can choose to buy in".
near-lab is where ideas get chosen, not built, and it never goes public.

**Decision.** `toothywalrus/near-keyhole` stands alone. Nothing here depends on reading near-lab. Testnet only; the owner
does every outward step.

**Consequences.** Facts copied from near-lab's research are restated here with their dates. Our own MIT code
that was adapted is listed in the README.
