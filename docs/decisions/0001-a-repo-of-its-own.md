# 0001: Keyhole is its own repo; the design comes from a private research repo

**Context.** A private research repo of ours chose this design for Illia Polosukhin's Nov 4 idea:
"private trading where you sell your viewing keys ... PnL can be publicly displayed so people can choose to buy in".
That repo is where ideas get chosen, not built, and it never goes public.

**Decision.** `toothywalrus/near-keyhole` stands alone. Nothing here depends on reading it. Testnet only; the owner
does every outward step.

**Consequences.** Facts copied from that research are restated here with their dates. Our own MIT code
that was adapted is listed in the README.

Edited 2026-11-10: the private repo's name and file paths removed; nothing else changed.
